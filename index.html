Semester Timetable & Faculty Slots

Build your own timetable: pick a theory faculty and their lab is selected automatically: slot 1 theory (A1, B1, F1…) gets an afternoon/evening lab, slot 2 theory (A2, B2, F2…) gets a morning lab. You can still change the lab by hand. Clashes are flagged in red with a message. Create several timetables with + New to compare options; they are saved in this browser. Click a selected row again to clear it.
